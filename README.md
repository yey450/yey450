### Hi, I'm Niki Ye 👋
**Autonomous Electron Microscopy & AI-Driven Characterization 🔬**

Developing **Foundation Model-powered Vision Pipelines**, **4D-STEM Quantitative Analytics**, and **Automated In-situ Workflows** for nanoscale materials.

---

#### ⚡ Core Stack
* **Microscopy & Physics:** `py4DSTEM` · `HyperSpy` · `Atomap` · `In-situ TEM/SEM` · `DM3/DM4 / EMD`
* **AI & Vision Foundation:** `PyTorch` · `SAM 2 (Segment Anything)` · `DINOv2` · `BoTorch (Active Learning)` · `Scikit-image`
* **High-Performance Computing:** `Python` · `CuPy (CUDA)` · `NumPy / SciPy` · `Napari`

---

#### 🚀 Research & Engineering Focus
* **Zero-Shot Nanoscale Segmentation:** Adapting vision foundation models (`SAM 2`) for low-dose, high-noise in-situ TEM video streams.
* **Sub-Pixel Drift & Dynamics Tracking:** Automated frame registration and kinetic quantification of phase transformations and interface evolution.
* **4D-STEM & Strain Mapping:** GPU-accelerated diffraction pattern analysis and geometric phase analysis (GPA).
